Frequently asked questions

Is there a free trial?

Yes — all plans include a 14-day free trial. No credit card required to start.

Can I change plans later?

Absolutely. Upgrade or downgrade at any time. Changes take effect on your next billing cycle.

Is my data secure?

Yes. All data is encrypted in transit and at rest. We use enterprise-grade infrastructure with daily automated backups.

Does it work on mobile?

PocketPortal is built mobile-first. Add it to your home screen from any browser — it works like a native app on iPhone and Android.

Is it specific to Louisiana?

PocketPortal was built with Louisiana's civil law framework, IOLTA rules, and ODC/LADB requirements in mind — but it works for attorneys in any state.

Can I white-label it for my firm?

Yes — Premium plan includes full white-labeling with your firm's colors, logo, and custom domain.
