One app. Your entire practice.

No more switching between five different tools. PocketPortal brings it all together.

💼

Case management

Track every matter from intake to close. Cases organized by practice area with deadlines, next actions, and financials at a glance.

🏦

IOLTA trust accounting

Per-client trust ledgers, three-way reconciliation reports, and compliance tools built for Louisiana Rule 1.15 and LADB/ODC requirements.

🗓

Smart calendar

Court dates, deadlines, depositions, and client meetings in one view. Add events with time, location, notes, and automatic staff notifications.

💬

Team messaging

Firm-wide channels, per-case threads organized by practice area, and direct messages — all in one secure place.

🧾

Billing & invoicing

Log time, generate branded invoices, and accept payments via LawPay — all from your phone. Time entries link directly to case financials.

Legal research library

AI-powered Louisiana statute search and case law research built right into your workflow. No separate login, no context switching.

Time clock

Staff clock in and out directly from the app. Track daily, weekly, and pay period hours. Admins see the whole team at a glance.
