Privacy Policy

Effective October 10, 2026 · Version 1.0

1. Who we are

PocketPortal is practice management software for law firms, operated by Hunter Legal, LLC (“we,” “us”). This policy explains what information we collect, how we use and protect it, and the choices you have. Questions can be sent to hunterlegal1822@outlook.com.

PocketPortal is used by law firms (“firms”) and their staff. A firm decides what client and matter information it puts into PocketPortal. We process that information on the firm’s behalf to provide the service, and we do not use it for our own purposes.

2. Information we collect

  • Account information: name, email address, firm name, role, and sign-in credentials for staff and invited users.
  • Firm content: cases, contacts, calendar events, messages, time entries, invoices, trust and operating ledger entries, documents, and other information a firm chooses to enter.
  • Demo and contact requests: the details you submit through our website forms, such as name, email, firm, and message.
  • Technical information: basic log data such as IP address, browser type, and timestamps, used to keep the service secure and working.
  • Billing information: if you subscribe, payment is handled by our payment processor. We do not store full card numbers.

3. Bank account information through Plaid

Where a firm chooses to connect a bank account, PocketPortal uses Plaid Inc. (“Plaid”) to retrieve read-only account and transaction information. Connecting is always the firm’s choice and happens only after a notice explaining what will be accessed.

  • Access is read-only. PocketPortal cannot move money or make changes to a bank account.
  • We never see or store your bank login credentials. You enter them directly with Plaid and your bank.
  • Imported transactions are held for staff review and are assigned to the correct ledger by an authorized user.
  • Access tokens are kept on our servers only, are not exposed to browsers or apps, and are deleted when a connection is removed.
  • Plaid’s handling of your information is described in the Plaid End User Privacy Policy.

You can disconnect a bank account at any time from within PocketPortal, or contact us and we will remove the connection.

4. How we use information

  • To provide, maintain, and support PocketPortal for your firm.
  • To authenticate users and keep each firm’s data separated from other firms.
  • To send service messages such as invitations, notifications, and security alerts.
  • To respond to demo, support, and contact requests.
  • To detect, investigate, and prevent security issues and misuse, and to meet legal obligations.

We do not sell personal information and we do not use firm or client data for advertising.

We share information only with service providers that help us operate PocketPortal, and only as needed for that purpose:

We may also disclose information if required by law, or to protect the rights, safety, and security of our users and the service. If PocketPortal is ever part of a merger or sale, we will require the new owner to honor this policy.

6. Security

Data is encrypted in transit and at rest. Access to firm data is limited by role and enforced at the database level, and we follow a written information security policy. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your information and will notify affected firms of a breach as required by law.

7. Retention and deletion

We keep firm data for as long as the firm has an account. After an account is closed, firm data is deleted within 90 days, and any bank connection tokens are deleted within 24 hours of disconnection or closure. Server logs are kept for up to 12 months, and website form messages for up to 24 months. Encrypted backups may persist for up to 7 days after deletion before they expire. Firms are responsible for exporting records they must retain under their own professional obligations before closing an account.

8. Your choices and rights

You may ask us to access, correct, export, or delete personal information we hold about you, or to disconnect a bank account. Because we process firm and client data on a firm’s behalf, requests about that data may need to be directed to the firm, and we will help the firm respond. Contact hunterlegal1822@outlook.com and we will respond within a reasonable time.

9. Children

PocketPortal is a business tool for law firms and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect their personal information.

10. Changes and contact

We may update this policy from time to time. We will post the updated version here with a new effective date, and notify firms of material changes. Contact us at hunterlegal1822@outlook.com.
