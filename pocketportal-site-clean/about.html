Why PocketPortal exists.

Solo practitioners and small law firms are the backbone of the American legal system. They handle the cases that matter most to real people — families, small businesses, individuals navigating the legal system for the first time.

Yet the tools available to them have always been built for big firms with big budgets and dedicated IT departments. They're expensive, complicated, and designed for someone else.

PocketPortal was built to change that. We believe every attorney — whether you're a solo practitioner in Baton Rouge or a three-person firm in Boise — deserves practice management software that's powerful, affordable, and actually fits the way you work.

We built PocketPortal from the inside out — starting with the specific compliance requirements of Louisiana attorneys (IOLTA, ODC, LADB), the civil law nuances that make Louisiana practice unique, and the reality that most attorneys run their practice from their phones.

The result is an app that handles everything from case management to trust accounting to legal research — in one place, on any device, without a six-figure technology budget.
